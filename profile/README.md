# Applied Intelligence Research Team

The **Applied Intelligence Research Team (ANS AIR Team)** is a research team hosted at the
**Faculty of Engineering Sciences, University of Applied Sciences in Nowy Sacz (ANS)**, focusing on
the development and practical deployment of **intelligent systems** in medicine, sport, robotics,
production engineering and mechatronics.

Our mission is to build transparent, quantitatively validated and reproducible intelligent systems
and to bring them into practice together with clinical, sports and industrial partners.

---

## Research focus

The team conducts research in areas including:
- Veterinary and medical image analysis, data pathology detection and annotation reliability
- Video-based analysis of movement technique in sport
- Humanoid and legged robotics: navigation, natural language interaction and pointing gestures
- Visual quality inspection and condition monitoring in production engineering
- Fault diagnosis and learning-based control of mechatronic systems

---

## Projects

Selected research projects developed within the team include:
- **Automatic TPA Evaluation** – automated measurement of the Tibial Plateau Angle (TPA)
  from pre- and post-TPLO radiographs in dogs, with detection of erroneous data and annotations
- **FightVision** – automatic analysis of techniques and combinations in Olympic boxing
  from video recordings, without wearable sensors
- **Campus Guide** – a humanoid robot that answers questions, guides visitors to a requested room
  and indicates it with a pointing gesture
- Ongoing and future projects in intelligent quality inspection and mechatronic diagnostics

Each project has one research lead, a team of two to five members and a duration of up to
12 months. Results of each academic year are summarised in an annual team monograph.

---

## Collaboration

The Applied Intelligence Research Team collaborates with **national and international
academic, clinical and industrial partners**, including:

- **University of Zurich**, Switzerland  
  *(Vetsuisse Faculty, University Animal Hospital)*
- **Medical Image Intelligence Lab, University of Rzeszow**, Poland
- **Faculty of Physical Culture Sciences and Safety, ANS**, Poland
- Sports clubs and regional manufacturing and automation companies

The team is open to students, student research clubs, doctoral candidates and external
collaborators.

---

## Data and ethics

All projects strictly follow institutional policies and applicable data protection
regulations (e.g. GDPR).

Clinical, imaging and athlete data are **not stored in public or version-controlled repositories**.
Access to data is provided exclusively through secure, institution-approved channels
to authorized collaborators.
